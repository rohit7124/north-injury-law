Common questions

General information to help you understand the process. For guidance on your specific situation, speak with an attorney directly.

Many personal injury attorneys, including our firm, work on a contingency basis — meaning you generally don't pay attorney's fees unless we recover compensation for you. Case costs and fee structures can vary, so we walk through the specifics during your free case evaluation.

Under a typical contingency arrangement, there's no upfront payment required to begin working with an attorney. The details of any fee agreement should always be reviewed and understood before you sign anything.

Deadlines for filing a claim, known as statutes of limitations, can vary depending on the type of case, the circumstances, and the jurisdiction. Because these deadlines can significantly affect your case, it's important to speak with a qualified attorney about your specific situation as soon as possible.

Generally speaking, priorities include seeking medical attention, documenting the scene if it's safe to do so, exchanging information with other involved parties, and reporting the incident as required. Because every situation is different, it's a good idea to discuss the specifics with an attorney soon after the incident.

Insurance companies may contact you soon after an accident, sometimes before you've had a chance to fully understand your situation. Many people find it helpful to speak with an attorney before providing a detailed statement, so they understand how their words might be used.

You share the basic details of what happened, either by phone or through our online form. An attorney reviews the information and discusses your potential options with you — there's no cost and no obligation to move forward.
